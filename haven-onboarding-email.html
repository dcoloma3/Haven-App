Let's get your community up and running 🏡

Here's everything you need to know to hit the ground running with Haven — in just a few minutes.

Welcome to Haven. As an administrator, you have access to everything your community needs — residents, medications, staff, certifications, incidents, and more. Here's a quick overview of the most important things to know.

1

Set up your Dashboard

Your Dashboard is the home base — it shows your total residents, pending medications, open incidents, and maintenance requests at a glance. Everything updates in real time.

2

Add your residents

Go to Residents and tap + Add Resident. Fill in their name, room number, date of birth, and admission date. You can add more details — emergency contacts, care notes, and a photo — anytime.

3

Set up medications

Open a resident's profile and go to the Medications tab. Add each medication with its dosage and frequency. When it's time to administer, use the Dispense page to log it — this builds a complete medication record automatically.

4

Add your staff

Go to Staff Directory to add team members. Then head to Certifications to track licenses — Haven will alert you automatically when any certification is about to expire.

5

Log incidents & maintenance

Use Incidents to document any falls, behavioral events, or medical concerns. Use Maintenance to track repair requests. Both keep a complete, dated history so nothing slips through the cracks.

Quick Tips

📱 Works on your phone. Haven is fully optimized for mobile — staff can dispense meds, log incidents, and check schedules right from their phone.
🏘️ Managing multiple communities? Use the community switcher in the top navigation to move between facilities — each one is completely separate and secure.
⚙️ Customize your facility. Go to Settings to update your community name, contact info, and preferences.
